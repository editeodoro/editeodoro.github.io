Galactic Dynamics & Orbit Families Theory Guide

1. Triaxial & Spherical Logarithmic Potential

The visualizer models orbits in a generalized logarithmic potential given by:

Φ(x, y, z) = ½ v₀² ln( r_c² + x² + y²/q₁² + z²/q₂² )

When q₁ = 1 and q₂ = 1, the potential reduces to the simple spherical logarithmic potential producing regular precessing rosette orbits.

2. Rotating Reference Frames & Jacobi Constant

When the bar rotates with pattern speed $\vec{\Omega}_p = (0, 0, \Omega_p)$, the equations of motion in the corotating frame include Coriolis and centrifugal accelerations:

a_x = -∂Φ/∂x + 2 Ω_p v_y + Ω_p² x
a_y = -∂Φ/∂y - 2 Ω_p v_x + Ω_p² y
a_z = -∂Φ/∂z

In a steady rotating frame, individual kinetic energy is not conserved, but the Jacobi Integral ($E_J$) is strictly conserved:

E_J = ½ (v_x² + v_y² + v_z²) + Φ(x,y,z) - ½ Ω_p² (x² + y²) = E_inertial - Ω_p L_z

3. Barred Orbit Families ($x_1, x_2, x_4$)

In rotating barred potentials (Contopoulos & Papayannopoulos 1980), non-axisymmetric torques support key orbit families:

  • x₁ Family: Stable orbits elongated parallel to the bar ($x$-axis).
  • x₂ Family: Stable orbits elongated perpendicular to the bar ($y$-axis), inside ILR.
  • x₄ Family: Stable retrograde loops circulating opposite to bar rotation.
  • Corotation Chaos: Resonant overlap near corotation radius $R_{CR} = v_0 / \Omega_p$.
